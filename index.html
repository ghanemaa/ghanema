Shipping & Returns

Shipping Policy

We deliver across Egypt. Estimated delivery takes 2–10 business days after order confirmation, depending on your governorate and courier availability. Delivery times are estimates and may change during busy periods or holidays.

Shipping fees are calculated automatically at checkout when you select your governorate. Please enter your area, full address and phone number accurately to help us deliver your order.

Unreceived Orders

If a customer refuses or fails to receive an order after dispatch without an error on GHANEMA's part, a 200 EGP non-delivery fee may apply, provided this charge was disclosed before the order was confirmed and is permitted by applicable law.

Returns & Exchanges

Return or exchange requests may be submitted within 3 days of receiving the order, without limiting any longer return rights provided by Egyptian law.

Items must be unused and in their original condition, with all original packaging and accessories.

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery so we can assist you promptly.

Returned items are subject to inspection to verify their condition.

For returns without a defect or an error by GHANEMA, return shipping costs are the customer's responsibility, where permitted by applicable law.

For confirmed manufacturing defects or incorrect orders, GHANEMA covers applicable return or exchange shipping costs.
